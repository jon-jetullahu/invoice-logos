# Questions for the CBK pre-application meeting

## A. Legal route and timing
1. Which law governs an application filed in Q4 2026 or Q1 2027: 08/L-304 or the transitional provisions of 04/L-093? Which implementing regulations have been re-issued under 08/L-304?
2. Can Pro Finance Home Sh.A., a CBK-licensed NBFI, apply to become a bank **as the same legal entity**? Or must it establish a new joint-stock company and transfer its portfolio? Is there a statutory portfolio-transfer mechanism?
3. Can the NBFI licence and activities continue in parallel during the licensing process? What happens to existing customer contracts, including the Islamic-principle contracts?
4. What are the current deadlines between preliminary approval and the final-licence application, and how long is a preliminary approval valid?

## B. Capital
5. What is the current minimum initial capital under 08/L-304? When must it be deposited, and in what form (cash at CBK)?
6. How will CBK treat the existing nominal capital of EUR 11.4m: as paid-in cash, as retained losses or earnings, or with deductions? Must the full minimum be **fresh cash**?
7. Which capital ratios apply to a de-novo bank: CET1, T1, total, buffers, any Pillar 2 add-on, and the leverage ratio? Does CBK expect a de-novo bank to hold a higher capital ratio for the first 3 years?
8. Which instruments are eligible as AT1 or T2 (for example, shareholder subordinated loans)? What are the dividend restrictions during the early years?

## C. Prudential and operational
9. Liquidity: does CBK apply LCR/NSFR or the 25% liquid-asset ratio? What is the current reserve requirement and its base?
10. Outsourcing: does the fully hosted and managed Facilization platform (Oracle-based, offshore delivery from Albania) require CBK approval? What are the requirements on data location, audit rights and exit?
11. Minimum governance: board size, the number of independent directors, the required committees, and the list of positions needing CBK approval.
12. Which functions may be outsourced or co-sourced (internal audit, IT security operations, DPO), and can the DPO or compliance roles be combined?
13. DIFK premium rate and timing; KIPS participation requirements and fees; credit-registry requirements.

## D. Islamic products
14. Does a bank licence permit an Islamic window? Is separate approval or a separate licence condition required?
15. Are Mudarabah investment accounts treated as deposits, and are they covered by the DIFK?
16. Are there CBK expectations for Sharia governance, disclosure, and the risk-weighting of Murabaha inventory and Ijarah assets?
17. Can the tax authority confirm the VAT and transfer-tax treatment of Murabaha?
18. Law 08/L-304 caps income from non-financial services at 10% of prior-year net interest income. Are Murabaha sale margins and Ijarah rentals treated as financial-services income for that cap?

## E. Cards and payments
19. Is a Visa Principal membership expected, or is an Affiliate/sponsored model acceptable to CBK? Are there requirements for card-processor outsourcing?
