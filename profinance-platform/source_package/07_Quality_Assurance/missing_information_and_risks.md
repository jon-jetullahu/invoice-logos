# Missing information and risk register

**Do not ask user to obtain additional internal Pro Finance data as a prerequisite.**
Instead support editable scenario assumptions and visible 'unknown' states.

Critical unknowns:
- Audited opening balance sheet, existing cash and liabilities, verified regulatory capital and RWA.
- Interest/financing yields, fees, deposits by product and funding costs.
- Credit losses, IFRS 9 methodology and impairment history.
- Employees, payroll, branches and other operating costs.
- Licence effective dates, bank go-live date and CBS subscription commencement.
- Capital injection timing and whether additional EUR 10m equity will be raised.
- Actual card adoption/transactions, issuer processor and merchant acquiring cost details.
- USD/EUR rate and tax treatment.
- Complete, validated historical reports of PriBank and other banks.
- Current CBK regulations and legal transition rules under 2026 law.

Prioritize transparency: model results should display 'Illustrative based on editable assumptions' and show missing-data flags. Do not claim CBK approval or bank feasibility on insufficient inputs.
