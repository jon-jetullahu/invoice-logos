# Facilization CBS — scope and exclusions

Original attached offer, dated **23 October 2026**, prepared for Pro Finance Home. Valid until **30 November 2026**. Indicative ranges, five-year subscription term, prices in EUR excluding VAT. Full bill of materials and detailed pricing promised after mutual NDA and CBK in-principle approval.

## Included as claimed by vendor
- Oracle-based, Islamic-ready core banking; goods financing and leasing; collateral and limits.
- Domestic and cross-border payments; credit registry connectivity; third-party API integration.
- Web/mobile banking; remote onboarding; e-signing; customer notifications.
- Customer 360, campaigns, automated loan origination.
- IFRS 9 provisioning; risk-weighted asset calculation; operational risk; AML and fraud monitoring; regulatory reporting.
- Hosting, application and infrastructure support, monitoring and releases.

## Explicitly excluded
- Card processing and scheme fees.
- SWIFT membership, CBK and registry fees.
- Office network and end-user IT.
- Domain, certificates and app stores.

## Critical clarifications
- Exact Oracle modules and third-party licence entitlements.
- Implementation timeline, payment milestones, subscription start date.
- Annual escalation, volume limits, internal user seat pricing, accounts/transactions tiering.
- Islamic finance functionality and accounting/legal implementation details.
- Data migration, testing, business continuity, cybersecurity, exit rights, data portability.
- Card management vs card processing vs card integration and any hidden additional fees.
- Tax/VAT treatment and accounting CAPEX vs OPEX.
