# MANDATORY CBK LICENSING, COST AND STAFFING WORKSTREAM — CLAUDE

This is a critical workstream, not an optional appendix. Complete it in parallel with audited bank report collection and before asserting Pro Finance bank-readiness. Deliver original official regulatory PDFs, effective-dated requirements database, cited legal memo, organization chart, HR headcount and salary-cost scenarios, and end-to-end bank conversion budget.

## 1. Legal applicability first
- Determine which Kosovo law governs the application on the exact filing date: verify Law No. 08/L-304 on Banks, its publication, effective date, transitional provisions and superseded Law No. 04/L-093. Do not infer legal effectiveness from PDF availability.
- Read CBK's 2020 Regulation on Licensing Banks and Branches, amendment of 23 Dec 2025, banking licensing manual and FAQs, and every linked operative regulation. Download original PDFs and maintain article/page-level citations, effective dates and change log.
- Clarify whether an existing licensed non-bank financial institution can convert into a bank by legal transformation, or must establish a separately licensed bank with asset/liability transfer. Research CBK precedents; label unresolved issues requiring CBK written confirmation.
- Official historical 2020 regulation stated EUR 7m minimum initial capital deposit, excluding premises, equipment and operating costs. THIS IS HISTORICAL, NOT CONFIRMED CURRENT LAW. Verify current threshold and any higher minimum, capital buffers and CBK supervisory discretion.

## 2. Licence application complete checklist
Produce a mandatory-vs-prudential-expectation-vs-good-practice matrix, with article citations, required evidence, owner, cost, time, dependency and completion status. Cover at minimum:
- Pre-application CBK meeting, forms, fees, deadlines, stages, in-principle vs final approval, onsite verification, expiry and conditions.
- Legal entity incorporation/changes, beneficial ownership, qualifying shareholders, source-of-funds, related parties, share transfers, group structure and investor approvals.
- Three-to-five-year business plan, strategy, product and deposit forecasts, funding plan, stress scenarios, financial statements and capital planning.
- Minimum paid-in capital, regulatory capital instruments, CET1/Tier 1/total capital, RWA, buffers, leverage, ICAAP, concentration, connected exposures, provisioning, liquidity, LCR/NSFR, required reserves and depositor protection — only as rules verified from current official texts.
- Governance: board size, independent directors, board committees, CEO, deputies, fit-and-proper documentation, qualifications, incompatibilities, integrity checks and prior CBK approvals.
- Control functions: risk, compliance, AML/CFT/MLRO, internal audit, finance/accounting, information security, data protection, credit, treasury and regulatory reporting; identify which must be independent, which may be outsourced and whether minimum headcount exists.
- Policies: risk appetite, credit/underwriting, collateral, provisioning/IFRS9, liquidity/ALM, outsourcing, cyber, incident response, BCP/DR, business continuity tests, consumer protection, complaints, fraud, sanctions, KYC, AML, related parties, remuneration, internal audit, procurement and data retention.
- Technology: CBS, customer onboarding, payments, credit registry, reporting, reconciliation, IT governance, security, penetration testing, backup/DR, infrastructure, SWIFT/SEPA applicability, cards, e-banking, audit trails, cybersecurity and third-party due diligence.
- Operations: premises, branches, cash vault, ATM/POS if chosen, deposit account administration, customer disclosures, payment systems and deposit insurance membership/fees.
- Islamic/goods financing and leasing: whether products and terminology require additional legal, accounting, governance, Sharia oversight or tax treatment; do not assume specific CBK permission.

## 3. HR organization and salary research
Create a defensible bank-ready operating model for 5,000/10,000/15,000 active accounts, 30/40/50 internal CBS seats and EUR 20m/40m/60m portfolio. IMPORTANT: internal system seats ARE NOT employee headcount.
- Define required vs recommended roles, qualifications, CBK approvals, reporting lines, segregation of duties, hiring phase, headcount and annual employer cost.
- Research actual Kosovo banking salaries from published job adverts, salary surveys, Kosovo statistics, CBK annual reports, bank financial statements personnel expenses/employee counts, recruiting sites and documented compensation disclosures.
- For each role, provide low/base/high monthly GROSS salary and TOTAL EMPLOYER COST in EUR, with source, date, sample size, confidence, taxes/pension employer burden and annual escalation. Never invent sourced salaries. If no role-specific reliable market evidence, mark `ESTIMATE_NOT_MARKET_VERIFIED` and justify using proxy/benchmark.
- Roles: chair/board fees, CEO, COO, CFO, CRO, compliance head, MLRO/AML officer, head of internal audit, CIO/CTO, CISO, data protection, head of credit, credit analysts, collections, treasury/ALM, financial controller, regulatory reporting, accounting, legal, HR, procurement, product, payments, card operations, operations/back office, branches, tellers, customer service, IT support, developers/integration, cybersecurity, fraud, KYC, risk analysts and facilities.
- Include branch-by-branch staffing and centralized/shared-service variants; explain independence constraints and outsourcing approval rules.
- Produce 3 HR scenarios: lean compliant, base and expansion; DO NOT claim the lean scenario is compliant without verified legal requirements and expert review.
- Compute employer payroll, bonuses, benefits, pension contributions, recruitment, training, licensing, board remuneration, consultant fees and annual inflation.

## 4. Full transformation cost stack
Separate mandatory costs, optional product expansion and estimated contingency. Estimate one-off, recurring annual, volume-based and regulatory fees, with original source currency and FX.
- Capital requirement is NOT an expense; show separately as locked/eligible capital and liquidity/funding.
- Legal incorporation/licensing, legal counsel, audit, external advisory, CBK application/supervision fees, deposit insurance membership and premiums, credit registry, payment system participation, minimum reserve, liquidity portfolio cost, security and cash logistics.
- CBS Facilization (original PDF supplied), onboarding/KYC, AML/fraud, IFRS9, RWA/regulatory reporting, cyber, DR, hosting, telecom, office IT, integration, digital channels, SWIFT and card issuing/acquiring. Avoid CBS/Visa/processor double counting.
- Premises, branches, fit-out, leases, insurance, furniture, cash handling, security, marketing, deposits acquisition and customer support.
- Show phased pre-licence, pre-launch, Year 1–3 cash flows and P&L impacts with accounting distinctions.
